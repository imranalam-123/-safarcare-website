Care beyond the ride

We believe every life matters.

SafarCare's mission includes working toward complimentary ambulance assistance for eligible customers through qualified local emergency-service partners.

  • Dedicated assistance pathway — a clear contact line and request process once operational.
  • Trusted local partners — qualified ambulance operators and verified coverage.
  • Transparent eligibility — clear service areas, limits and terms before launch.
Medical emergency? This demo cannot dispatch an ambulance. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance or 108 for ambulance services where available. Do not wait for a website response. Free ambulance coverage is a proposed service and is not active through this demo.
Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Does this website accept real bookings?

No. This is a front-end demo. It creates a local booking preview but does not send requests to drivers or save information to a server.

How much does a ride cost?

Fares are not configured. A real service should confirm prices based on route, distance, vehicle, timing, tolls and applicable fees.

Is the ambulance service active and free?

No. It is a proposed benefit. Before advertising it as available, arrange qualified providers, funding, coverage, dispatch procedures and clear terms.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes, the website is responsive and designed for both mobile and desktop browsers.
